Poring sprite — animation preview

Eight frames from row 2 of resources/poring-sheet-orange.webp (happy/idle) and resources/poring-sheet-green.webp (sad), each re-centered into uniform 240×200 cells with the same baked-in bounce (frames 1-3 squish, frame 7 peaks at -24px, frames 6 & 8 flank at -16px). Plays at 12 fps in a 667 ms loop.

1. CSS sprite-strip — production approach (12 fps)

The actual UI uses this. .poring.sad swaps the strip URL to the green sprite while preserving the exact same animation and bounce — only the background image changes.
